Avery L7171 · 200 × 60 mm · 4 per A4 sheet

KLBD Archive spine labels

All thirty archive files, laid out at true size across eight sheets. Each label reads the right way up once it is on a binder standing on the shelf. The page prints landscape, which lays the labels along the sheet to match the die-cut. Settings below are for Chrome or Edge on Windows, printing to the Lexmark XC9255.

In Chrome or Edge on the PC
01

Press Ctrl + P and pick the Lexmark XC9255. Open More settings.

02

Tick Background graphics. Without it the navy panels print blank — this is the one setting people miss.

03

Set Scale to Custom → 100. Chrome’s “Default” scale quietly shrinks the page to fit the printable area, which throws the alignment out by a few millimetres.

04

Set Layout to Landscape — this is what turns the artwork the right way up along the spine on a portrait A4 label sheet. Set Margins to None, Paper size to A4, and untick Headers and footers.

At the Lexmark
05

Load the label sheets in the multipurpose / bypass tray on the side, not in a paper cassette. Cassettes bend the sheet tightly and can peel labels off inside the machine.

06

On the touchscreen set that tray’s paper type to Labels and size to A4. This slows the feed and adjusts the fuser — running labels as plain paper is what causes jams and adhesive on the rollers.

07

The XC9255 takes A3, so double-check it hasn’t auto-picked an A3 tray. If the machine holds jobs for badge or PIN release, release it there as usual.

Before you commit the label stock
08

Print one test on plain paper and measure the 100 mm bar printed in the margin, outer edge to outer edge. If it reads short (96–98 mm is typical), the printer is shrinking the page — find and turn off “fit to printable area” in the driver. Nothing else will line up until that bar measures 100.

09

Then lay that test over a label sheet at a window. The five ticks along the top and bottom margins should sit exactly on the die-cut lines between labels. If the bar reads 100 but the ticks drift, tell me by how much at the last tick and I’ll correct the spacing.

10

Feed label sheets one at a time. Peel each label and apply it down the binder spine, number at the top. If the print lands a millimetre out, adjust --offset-x / --offset-y in the source file. Sheet 1 carries files 1–4, sheet 2 files 5–8, and so on.
